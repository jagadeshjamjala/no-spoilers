# Senior Data Engineer, Payments Data Platform — Ledgerly (fictional)

> Fictional company and JD, written for examples and tests.

**About us.** Ledgerly provides payment processing and treasury tools for 40,000 small and mid-size businesses across the EU and US. We process about 3M payment events per day, peaking at 1,500 events/sec.

**The role.** You'll join the 6-person Payments Data Platform team. You'll own the pipelines that turn raw payment events into the ledger-accurate datasets that finance, risk and merchant-facing reporting depend on.

**What you'll do**
- Own batch and streaming pipelines from our payment services (Kafka) to our lakehouse (S3 + Apache Iceberg) and warehouse (Snowflake).
- Design data models for the ledger, settlements, refunds and disputes that finance can reconcile to the cent.
- Lead the migration of legacy SSIS reconciliation jobs to dbt + Airflow.
- Define data-quality SLAs and build the observability to meet them.
- Mentor two mid-level engineers, and review designs across the team.

**You have (must-have)**
- 5+ years in data engineering, including owning production pipelines.
- Expert SQL and strong Python.
- Data modeling for financial or transactional data (SCDs, fact grain, reconciliation).
- Hands-on Kafka or a similar streaming platform, including handling duplicates and late events.
- Airflow and dbt in production.
- Snowflake or a comparable cloud warehouse, including cost management.

**Nice to have**
- Apache Iceberg or Delta Lake.
- Spark (PySpark).
- Experience with PCI-DSS or other PII-sensitive data.
- Experience leading a legacy-to-cloud migration.

**Interview process**
Recruiter screen → SQL live exercise → data modeling → system design → behavioral with the hiring manager.
